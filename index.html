    How to read the numbers

    • Each card shows the nearest mapped NAB station, not a block-by-block reading.
    • Station counts are observed counts, so dates can lag during off-season or sparse reporting periods.
    • Tree and grass pollen are often highest in the evening during spring and summer.
    • If symptoms keep breaking through, an allergist can help tune meds or immunotherapy.
